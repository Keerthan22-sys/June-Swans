THE DESIGNER

Fashion is personal. Every stitch should tell your story.

June Swans is the studio of Smitha — a fashion designer who believes that clothing should fit your body, your personality, and the moment you're dressing for. No two orders are the same because no two people are.

From custom ethnic wear for weddings and festivals to contemporary silhouettes for everyday elegance, every piece is hand-designed and crafted at her studio with meticulous attention to fabric, fit, and finish.

HOW IT WORKS

From your idea to your wardrobe, in four steps

01

Share your vision

Send a WhatsApp message or visit the studio. Share reference images, fabric preferences, and the occasion — Smitha takes it from there.

02

Design & fabric

Receive a personalised sketch with fabric recommendations. Refinements happen until the design feels exactly right.

03

Craft & stitch

Your outfit is hand-cut and stitched at the studio. Progress updates keep you in the loop throughout.

04

Perfect fit

A trial fitting ensures everything sits perfectly. Final adjustments, if any, are done before your piece is ready to wear.
